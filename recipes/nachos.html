Delicious nachos

Home

Description

These nachos are loaded with cheese, jalapenos, and your favorite toppings. Perfect for a snack or party!

Ingredients

  • 1 bag tortilla chips
  • 2 cups shredded cheddar cheese
  • 1/2 cup sliced jalapenos
  • 1/2 cup diced tomatoes
  • 1/4 cup sliced black olives
  • 1/4 cup chopped green onions
  • Sour cream and guacamole for serving

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C).
  2. Spread tortilla chips on a baking sheet.
  3. Sprinkle shredded cheese evenly over the chips.
  4. Add jalapenos, tomatoes, olives, and green onions on top.
  5. Bake for 10-15 minutes until cheese is melted and bubbly.
  6. Serve hot with sour cream and guacamole.
