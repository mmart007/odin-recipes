Description

This is the world's best lasagna recipe. It's easy to make and tastes amazing!

Ingredients

  • 12 lasagna noodles
  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 jar marinara sauce
  • 15 oz ricotta cheese
  • 2 cups mozzarella cheese
  • 1/4 cup parmesan cheese

Steps

  1. Cook lasagna noodles according to package instructions.
  2. Brown ground beef in a large skillet.
  3. Add marinara sauce and simmer for 10 minutes.
  4. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
  5. Layer noodles, meat sauce, and cheeses in a baking dish.
  6. Bake for 25-30 minutes until bubbly and golden.
