Oooey gooey Sheppard's Pie

Home

Description

This is the ultimate Sheppard's Pie recipe. It's hearty, comforting, and perfect for a family dinner!

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 cups frozen mixed vegetables
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 cup beef broth
  • 2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 4 cups mashed potatoes
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C).
  2. In a large skillet, cook ground beef and onion until browned.
  3. Add mixed vegetables, tomato paste, beef broth, and Worcestershire sauce. Simmer for 5-10 minutes until thickened.
  4. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
  5. Transfer the meat mixture to a baking dish and spread mashed potatoes evenly on top.
  6. Bake for 20-25 minutes until the top is golden brown.
  7. Let it cool for a few minutes before serving. Enjoy your delicious Sheppard's Pie!
